YOUR GROWING JOURNEY

New to Hydroponics? Start Here.

Hydroponics grows plants without traditional soil by delivering water and nutrients to their roots. Follow these simple steps to begin.

01

Choose Your Space

Select a balcony, indoor shelf or sunny growing area.

02

Select Your System

Try a simple water-based kit or a compact growing tower.

03

Add Water & Nutrients

Follow the system instructions and nutrient label.

04

Monitor & Harvest

Check water levels, plant health and light regularly.
