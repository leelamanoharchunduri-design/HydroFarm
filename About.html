GROW WITHOUT SOIL

What Is Hydroponic Farming?

Hydroponics is a method of growing plants without traditional soil. Plant roots receive water and essential mineral nutrients through a nutrient solution or another suitable growing medium.

How Does Hydroponics Work?

Instead of relying on soil to supply nutrients, hydroponic systems deliver essential minerals through water. Growers monitor water quality, nutrient concentration, light, temperature, and oxygen around the roots.

Depending on the system, plants may grow in channels, containers, net pots, or inert growing media such as coco coir, clay pebbles, and rockwool.

Common Hydroponic Systems

Deep Water Culture

Plant roots sit in nutrient-rich water with oxygen supplied to support root health.

Nutrient Film Technique

A thin, continuously flowing nutrient solution passes along plant roots in channels.

Ebb and Flow

The growing area is periodically flooded with nutrient solution and then drained.

Drip Systems

Nutrient solution is delivered to individual plants through small irrigation lines.

MAKE SPACE WORK SMARTER

Discover the Power of Vertical Farming

Vertical farming involves growing crops in vertically stacked layers, towers, or specially designed structures. It helps growers make better use of limited floor space and can be combined with hydroponic systems.

These growing environments may be installed indoors, in greenhouses, on balconies, or in other suitable locations. Indoor facilities can use LED grow lights and climate-control equipment to manage growing conditions.

Grow in Different Spaces

Explore suitable setups for homes, balconies, greenhouses, and commercial facilities.

Use Vertical Space

Stack growing levels or use towers to increase growing capacity within a limited footprint.

Manage Growing Conditions

Adjust lighting, irrigation, nutrients, and temperature to suit the crop and environment.

WHY MODERN GROWING?

Growing More Thoughtfully

Hydroponic and vertical farming can offer practical advantages when systems are designed and managed for the right crops, space, and local conditions.

Thoughtful Water Use

Recirculating hydroponic systems can reuse water and reduce losses compared with some soil-based growing methods. Results depend on system design and management.

Space Efficiency

Vertical towers and stacked growing levels can help increase the number of plants grown in a limited area.

Greater Control

Growers can monitor nutrients, water, light, and environmental conditions to support consistent growth.

Fresh Home Produce

Suitable systems can help people grow herbs, leafy greens, and other crops close to where they live.

Technology-Enabled Growing

Timers, sensors, LED lighting, and automated irrigation can help growers monitor and manage their systems.

Resource Awareness

Thoughtful planning can help manage water, nutrients, energy, and growing materials more responsibly.

Sustainability depends on factors such as electricity sources, crop selection, equipment, water management, and operating practices.
